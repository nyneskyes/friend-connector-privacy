Privacy Policy for Friend Connector

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Friend Connector (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is a simple app that shows conversation-starter questions. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. We’ve tried to keep it short and plain because the app collects very little.

Information stored on your device

Your settings — the theme you pick, the app icon you choose, your notification preference, and which question categories are enabled — are saved only on your device. This information never leaves your phone and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Notifications

If you turn on daily notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a server that sends you notifications, and we do not collect any data through them.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use the in-app feedback form, the message you write is sent to us through a third-party service called Formspree (formspree.io) so that we can read it and improve the app. When you submit feedback, Formspree processes your message and may collect limited technical information such as your IP address and the time of submission. We ask you not to include passwords or personal information in feedback. We do not ask for your name or email. Formspree’s handling of this data is governed by its own privacy policy: https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/

What we do NOT collect

We want to be clear about this. The app does not:

  • require an account or ask for your name, email, or phone number;
  • collect analytics or usage/tracking data;
  • collect your location;
  • use advertising or third-party trackers;
  • collect device identifiers or browsing history.

Children’s privacy

Friend Connector is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and revise the “Last updated” date above.
